Sibeve Group Donates Vests to Green Mamba Ladies Football Club

September 24, 2026 4 min read
← Back to Blog

At Sibeve Group, we believe branding is about more than logos and merchandise — it's about showing up for the communities that make our work meaningful. That's why we were proud to donate a set of custom vests to Green Mamba Ladies Football Club, supporting the team both on and off the pitch.

A Donation With Purpose

Women's football continues to grow in Eswatini, and clubs like Green Mamba Ladies are at the heart of that growth — building confidence, discipline, and community through sport. When the opportunity came to support their journey, our team jumped at the chance to put our sublimation and branding expertise to good use.

The vests were produced using our high-quality sublimation printing process, designed to be durable, vibrant, and built to handle the demands of match day. Handing them over was a proud moment for our team — a reminder of why we do what we do.

Why We Support Local Sport

  • Building Community: Local sports clubs bring people together and create a sense of belonging that strengthens neighborhoods and towns.
  • Empowering Women in Sport: Supporting women's football helps break down barriers and create more opportunities for female athletes in Eswatini.
  • Encouraging Discipline and Teamwork: Sport teaches life skills that extend far beyond the pitch, especially for young athletes.
  • Showcasing Local Talent: A well-branded, professional-looking team boosts morale and pride, both for players and supporters.
  • Living Our Values: Giving back to the community is core to how we do business at Sibeve Group.

Looking Ahead

This donation is part of Sibeve Group's ongoing commitment to giving back to the communities across Eswatini that support our business. From corporate branding to community sponsorships, we believe a strong brand is one that invests in the people around it.

We wish the Green Mamba Ladies Football Club every success this season, and we look forward to cheering them on. If your organization, club, or team is looking for high-quality branded apparel — whether for competition, training, or everyday wear — get in touch with us to see how we can help bring your vision to life.
